# Contextual Compression

## 1. The Retrieval Paradox

You retrieve 10 documents because you want **High Recall**.
But now your context window is full of irrelevant noise.
If the answer is in the 7th document, the LLM might miss it ("Lost in the Middle").

## 2. The Compressor

A **Contextual Compressor** sits between the Retriever and the LLM.
It takes the 10 retrieved docs and asks:
*"Given the user query, what parts of these docs are actually useful?"*

It acts like a highlighter pen.

## 3. Techniques

### A. LLMChainExtractor
Uses a small, fast LLM to rewrite the chunk.
*   **Original:** "The CEO, John Smith, announced earnings today. He also mentioned he likes golf. Revenue is up 5%."
*   **Query:** "What is the revenue?"
*   **Compressed:** "Revenue is up 5%."

### B. EmbeddingsFilter
Embeds the query and the sentences within the chunk. Only keeps sentences with high similarity.
Cheaper than LLM extraction.

## 4. Summary

Retrieval gets the document. Compression extracts the answer.
This creates a higher quality "Context Density" for the final generation model.

> **Key Intuition:** "Don't pay for tokens you don't need."
